Chapter two / the year we became family

This year, I call you wife.

My phone is always ringing at exactly the right time. Whether it is me calling the second I walk away from work, or you calling just as you are heading out, those calls are my favourite part of every single day. Even when it is late, even when our time zones are fighting against us, you are still the first person I want to talk to. Through a screen and thousands of miles of distance, you are still the one person who can instantly make me laugh with our endless teasing.

A letter for the birthday girl

My love, my person, my home.

Distance tries to make things hard, but you make our love look effortless. I see how strong you are for us, Ayanfe. I see the quiet ways you carry the weight of this separation, how hard you try to be the best wife you can be, and the way you lift us up in your du'as over and over again. You do not just love me; you protect our future with your prayers.

The grand moments of marriage are wonderful, but what I cherish most are the ordinary realities of us: the comfort of knowing we are on the exact same team, the way you soften the hardest days with just the sound of your voice, and the quiet joy of building a life that belongs to both of us.

On your birthday, my hands are raised in du'a for you. I pray Allah rewards every single prayer you have made for us and gives you the kind of year your beautiful heart deserves: a peace that stays, a joy that finds you easily, radiant health, endless barakah, and dreams that open at the right time. May He keep us growing with tenderness, patience, faith, and plenty of reasons to laugh at ourselves and each other.

Thank you for being my wife. Thank you for your strength, your heart, and your relentless love across this ocean.

I loved you while we were miles apart last year. I love you today as my wife, and I am counting down the seconds until I never have to say goodbye through a screen again.

Happy birthday, Ayanfe. Forever yours.
